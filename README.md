# Riwayyat

Idea:
Riwayyat is a digital platform dedicated to preserving and promoting India's rich cultural heritage. It enables users to discover traditional art forms, historical monuments, festivals, folklore, and local traditions through an interactive and accessible experience. The goal is to connect people with their roots while making cultural knowledge engaging for future generations.

Problem Statement:
India's diverse cultural heritage is scattered across multiple sources, making it difficult for people—especially younger generations and tourists—to access authentic and organized information. Many regional traditions, stories, and historical facts risk being forgotten due to limited digital preservation and awareness.

Solution:
Riwayyat provides a centralized platform where users can explore India's cultural heritage in one place. By combining information about traditions, historical sites, festivals, and local stories with an intuitive interface, the platform makes learning about culture simple, engaging, and accessible. Riwayyat encourages cultural awareness, preservation, and appreciation through technology.
